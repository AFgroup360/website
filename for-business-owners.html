Three levels of involvement.

Some businesses need a clearer monthly view. Others need someone working in the numbers through the month. You pick the level, and it can change as the business does.

Ongoing

Financial visibility

A team already runs the business day to day. What is missing is a clear, current picture of where it stands.

  • Management reporting you can act on
  • Cash visibility and forecasting
  • A recurring review of the numbers
  • A straight answer on the financial position
Ongoing

Financial control

We are in the numbers with you through the month, not only reporting on it afterwards.

  • Everything in financial visibility
  • Cash and payments run in month
  • Working capital actively managed
  • Financial controls and process in place
  • Support on decisions as they come up
When it is needed

Financing and growth readiness

Something significant is being decided. Financing, investment, an expansion or a sale.

  • Everything in financial control
  • The Financing Readiness Review
  • Preparation for lender and investor conversations
  • Modelling for major financial decisions

Scope and fees are set after an introductory call, once we know what the business actually needs.

Also available to you
