Privacy policy

AmeriFinancial provides outsourced financial control services. We take the confidentiality of your business and financial information seriously, because it is the foundation of the work. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how it is protected.

What we collect

We collect the information you give us directly. Your name, business name, email address, and any message you send through the contact form.

How we use it

Your information is used only to deliver our services. Responding to inquiries, preparing financial reporting, and communicating with you about your engagement. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Confidentiality

Information and documents you share with us are treated as confidential and used only to deliver the engaged services. We are happy to enter into a mutual NDA on request.

Retention

We keep client information for as long as your engagement requires and remove it on request. You may ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact hello@ameri-group.ca and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@ameri-group.ca. AmeriFinancial is based in Mississauga, Ontario.
