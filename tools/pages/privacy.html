Privacy Policy

AmeriFinancial ("we", "us") provides outsourced finance-control services. We take the confidentiality of your business and financial information seriously — it is the foundation of our work. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how it is protected.

What we collect

We collect the information you provide directly: your name, business name, email address, and any messages you send through our contact form. If you use our client portal, we also store the documents you upload and basic records of when they were provided.

How we use it

Your information is used solely to deliver our services: responding to inquiries, managing your document checklist, preparing financial reporting, and communicating with you about your engagement. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Confidentiality

Information and documents you share with us are treated as confidential and used only for delivering the engaged services. We are happy to enter into a mutual NDA on request.

Document security

Documents uploaded through the client portal are stored in secured cloud storage and are accessible only to you and AmeriFinancial's principals. Access requires a verified sign-in, and every download is gated by per-document authorization checks.

Retention

We keep client documents for as long as your engagement requires, and remove stored files when a document request or client account is deleted. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact hello@ameri-group.ca and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@ameri-group.ca. AmeriFinancial is based in Canada.
