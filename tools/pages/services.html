The deliverable

The Financing Readiness Report.

So nobody's deciding off whatever the business chose to show them.

Most of what goes wrong in a lending decision was sitting there the whole time. In the bank statements. In a debt nobody mentioned. In the gap between what got reported and what was really going on. The report puts all of it on the table before anyone signs, with a number on it. So the cash gap is something you can price, not something you find out about later.

Two years of cash flow. Where the balance sheet really stands. What they owe and to whom. How much they actually need. And what we'd want fixed before the money moves.
