The deliverable

The Financing Readiness Report.

So a decision is not made on whatever the business chose to show.

Most of what goes wrong in a credit decision was knowable at the time. It was sitting in the bank statements, or in a liability nobody mentioned, or in the gap between what was reported and what was actually happening. The report puts that on the table before anyone commits, and it puts a number on it, so the cash gap is a figure you can price rather than a risk carried blind.

Cash flow trending, balance sheet position, debt and obligation structure, the financing requirement as we assess it, and the conditions we would attach before funding.
