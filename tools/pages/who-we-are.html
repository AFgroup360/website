The work

What that looks like in practice.

Anonymised, and typical of the situations the firm is brought into.

A business preparing for financing

The owner needed capital. The numbers on hand were built to file a tax return, not to support a credit decision.

The situation
  • No reliable statement of the real financial position
  • Obligations and arrears not fully mapped
  • Nobody could say what the business could realistically service
What we did
  • Established the position from source documents
  • Completed a Financing Readiness Review
  • Identified the issues affecting financeability
  • Helped shape a structure the business could carry
What improved
  • The financing conversation ran on numbers both sides trusted
  • Conditions were known and addressed before they were imposed
  • We stayed on afterwards for the reporting and financial control

A growing business whose finance function had not kept pace

Revenue was climbing. The reporting, systems and controls were still the ones built when the business was half the size.

The situation
  • Month end arriving too late to be useful
  • No forward view of cash
  • Working capital absorbing more with every new job
  • Bigger decisions being made on thinner information
What we did
  • Built cash forecasting the business could rely on
  • Put in a monthly close that held
  • Worked receivables, payables and terms properly
  • Set controls to match the size the business had reached
What improved
  • Management got a current view of the business
  • Growth could be funded deliberately rather than absorbed
  • A finance function able to carry the next stage

Some of this work starts with the owner. Some of it starts with the capital provider. What gets built is much the same either way.
