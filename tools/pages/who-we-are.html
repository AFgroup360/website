The work

What that looks like in practice.

A business preparing for financing

The situation

The owner needed capital. The numbers on hand were built to file a tax return, not to support a credit decision, and nobody could say what the business could realistically carry.

What we did

Went into the business, established the real financial position, and completed a Financing Readiness Review. Identified the issues affecting financeability and helped shape a structure the business could service.

What improved

The financing conversation ran on numbers both sides trusted. We stayed on afterwards, providing the ongoing reporting and financial control the owner and the capital provider both wanted to see.

A growing business whose finance function had not kept up

The situation

Revenue was climbing but the reporting, controls and processes were still the ones built when the business was half the size. Management was making bigger decisions on thinner information.

What we did

Built the finance layer underneath the growth. Cash forecasting, a monthly close that held, working capital worked properly, and controls that matched the size the business had reached.

What improved

Management got a current view of the business and the confidence to keep growing, with a finance function that could carry the next stage rather than being outrun by it.
