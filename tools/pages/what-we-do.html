The deliverable

The Financing Readiness Review.

One document stating where the business stands and what would have to be true for capital to go in. It reaches a verdict rather than leaving the reader to form one.

{{REVIEW}}

What is inside it.

The period reviewed is set by the business and what the financing is for, not by a fixed template.

  1. 1 The verdict Financeable, financeable on conditions, or not in the current position.
  2. 2 Cash position and operating cash flow What the business generated, with owner and related party movement separated.
  3. 3 Working capital Receivables, payables, inventory, and the gap the business funds.
  4. 4 Debt and obligations Loans, leases, arrears and suppliers, with terms and where each ranks.
  5. 5 What the financing is for The use of funds, sized against the gap we measured.
  6. 6 Affordability What the business can service through a normal year, not a good one.
  7. 7 Conditions What a lender would reasonably attach, set out before they attach it.
  8. 8 Confirmed, and still open Figures tied to source documents. Anything that could not be is disclosed.
