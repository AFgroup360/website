Better visibility makes a healthier business.

That is worth the same to the person running the business and to the person financing it. Most of the work underneath is the same work.

Two perspectives, one philosophy

Same work. Different reason for wanting it.

We do not run two businesses. A business that is better understood and better controlled is the outcome either way.

If you run the business

Control of the decision.

You know the business better than anyone. What is missing is a current view of what it is doing, and someone to think it through with.

  • A current picture, not a year old one
  • Cash visible far enough ahead to act
  • Someone in the numbers alongside you
If you are financing one

Confidence in the position.

You are backing a business you cannot see inside every day. The same reporting gives you a reliable read on it.

  • Reporting that arrives without being chased
  • A clear read on the financial position
  • Discipline that holds after the deal closes
What we look at

The diagnostic starts in the same places.

Whatever the engagement, and whoever asked for it, these are the areas that tell you the most about a business the fastest.

What is happening now
  • Cash What is committed, what is coming in, and where the position lands.
  • Working capital The gap between paying suppliers and getting paid.
  • Margin Where it is earned and where it leaks, job by job.
Whether the numbers hold
  • Reporting quality Does it reconcile, arrive in time, and separate operating performance.
  • Financial controls Who approves what, and whether anything can move unseen.
What the business can take on
  • Debt and obligations Amounts, terms, and the order they rank in.
  • Use of funds What money is for, sized to the real gap.
  • Financing readiness Whether the position holds up read cold from outside.
How a month runs

The ongoing work is a cycle, not a project.

This is what keeps the numbers worth something on the day a decision has to be made. Financing work sits outside it, and comes up when it comes up.

  1. Through the month

    Monitor

    Cash, upcoming obligations, what cleared and what is about to.

  2. Month end

    Close

    Reconciled and tied out, so the month can be relied on.

  3. Then

    Report

    Performance against expectation, and where margin went.

  4. Together

    Review

    The few things that actually need a decision this month.

  5. Before it restarts

    Forecast

    Rebuilt on what happened, not on what was planned.

Then it begins again, with the forecast carrying forward what the month just taught it.

Working capital, drawn

The gap a business funds itself.

Win more work and you fund more of these gaps at the same time. The margin per job has not moved. The cash required has.

It is the most common reason a profitable business ends up needing money in a hurry, and it is visible months ahead if somebody is watching for it.
