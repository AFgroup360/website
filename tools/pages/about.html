About

Why AmeriFinancial exists.

AmeriFinancial was created to give business owners the financial leadership, insight and guidance they need to make better decisions and grow at a pace the business can carry.

We work alongside owners and their leadership teams to improve profitability, strengthen cash flow, sharpen financial visibility, and build confidence in the decisions that shape the future of the business.

Every owner should be able to answer three things on any given day. Where the cash is, what it is doing, and what happens next. Our job is to make sure they can.

Founder

Farid Ameri

Farid has run finance from the inside of struggling businesses, built finance departments where there were none, and sat on the lending side of the table deciding whether a deal got funded. The through line is the same everywhere. Get the cash under control, get the numbers straight, then make the decision.

Where the experience comes from.

  • Uganda Built a finance department from nothing for a business that had never had one, then renegotiated supplier terms so the day suppliers got paid lined up with the day customers paid. The cash gap closed, borrowing came down and so did the interest bill.
  • Tunis Ran the finance side day to day for a producer in real trouble. Cash first: what is in the bank, what leaves this week, who gets paid. The lenders got straight numbers and one person to call, and management got a report they could believe.
  • Inside a major Canadian bank Years on the side of the table that decides whether a file gets funded. That is where you learn what a credit request has to survive, and why the strong businesses still get turned down.
  • A manufacturer through a liquidity crunch Came in when the money was tight, rebuilt the forecast around what was actually coming and going, and kept the business trading while it worked its way out.

If you run the business, that is someone who has been in your seat when it was tight. If you fund one, it is someone who has read the file from your side too.
