FAQ

How are you different from a bookkeeper?

A bookkeeper records what already happened, and we work on what comes after.

Do you replace my accountant?

No. Your accountant keeps year end and tax, and we sit between your bookkeeper and your accountant.

What does a month look like?

Once a month we go through the cash position, the margins and the management report with you, working from read access to the books you already keep.
