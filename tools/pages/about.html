About

What we've actually done.

AmeriFinancial is a finance firm that sits between your bookkeeper and your accountant: cash you can see coming, reporting you can run on, and numbers a bank can work with.

Somebody has to look at the cash before it's gone, get a straight number in front of the owner every month, and keep the bank calm. The hard part is doing that when the business is under pressure. We've done it in three very different places. Here's the shape of the work, with the names left out.

A food producer, Tunisia

It was in trouble. We steadied it.

Cash was going out faster than anyone was tracking it, the lenders wanted answers, and nobody inside could pull a number worth trusting. Every conversation started with a guess. We came in and ran the finance side day to day. Cash first: what is in the bank, what leaves this week, who gets paid. The lenders got straight numbers and one person to call. It held. Management could open a report and believe it, and that is the floor everything else gets built on.

A family business, Uganda

Suppliers got paid when customers paid.

No finance department, so watching the money was nobody's job. They paid suppliers before their own customers paid them, borrowed to cover the stretch, and paid interest on every day of it. We built the finance department from nothing, then sat down with the suppliers and renegotiated terms so the day a supplier got paid lined up with the day a customer paid. The gap closed, the business borrowed less, and the interest bill came down. It was never a weak business. Just a timing problem nobody was minding.

Owner-led businesses, Canada

Grew faster than the books did.

Two files, closer to home, and nobody did anything wrong in either. One business was growing faster than its books: money leaking on things it no longer used, and a financing request sized wrong for any lender. The other could not see next month's cash. We found the leaks and put a price on them, rebuilt the ask at a size the business could actually carry, and built a forecast the owner looks at. One can now take a financing conversation seriously. The other sees a tight month before it lands.

Three places, three problems, one job underneath: get the cash under control, get the numbers right, keep the business moving.

Bookkeeping looks back. We look forward.

Once a month we go through the cash position, the margins and the management report with you, working from read access to the books you already keep. Nothing gets migrated and nothing gets disrupted. Your business is the boat. We're the floaties strapped to the side, keeping it from wobbling until it gets where it's going.
