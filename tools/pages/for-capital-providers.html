Before the money goes in, and after.

First to understand the business properly. Then to keep it visible and under control while your capital is in it.

Before funding

Understand the business.

A Financing Readiness Review, built from the company's own source records rather than from what has been reported.

  • The real financial position
  • Financial risks surfaced early
  • Financeability and affordability
  • Debt and obligations, with terms and priority
  • What the financing is actually for
  • Conditions that may need addressing first

The transaction Capital is deployed

After funding

Keep it visible and controlled.

The position stays current, so nobody has to wait for a year end to find out how the business is doing.

  • The same monthly reporting the owner runs on
  • Cash watched against the plan
  • Working capital kept under control
  • Early warning when the position moves
  • Confirmation that conditions are being met
  • A business that stays financeable

This is not oversight of the borrower. A business with better financial control is a stronger business, which is what both sides wanted from the start.

The deliverable

The Financing Readiness Review.

Where the business stands, what it can carry, and what would have to be addressed before capital moves. It reaches a verdict rather than leaving you to form one.
