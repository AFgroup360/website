After funding

Keep it visible and controlled.

The position stays current, so nobody has to wait for a year end to find out how the business is doing.

  • The same monthly reporting the owner runs on
  • Cash watched against the plan
  • Working capital kept under control
  • Early warning when the position moves
  • Confirmation that conditions are being met
  • A business that stays financeable

This is not oversight of the borrower. A business with better financial control is a stronger business, which is what both sides wanted from the start.
