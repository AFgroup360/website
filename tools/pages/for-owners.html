One we ran, with the details removed.

Illustrative. Client details withheld.
The business
Owner led, profitable, revenue up roughly 50% in a year. Payroll by hand, no accounting system, nobody whose job was the numbers.
What we found
A balance sheet account at a large negative balance that cannot be negative. Years of uncategorised transactions in one place. Any lender who opens that file closes it.
The leak
Roughly five figures a month going out for capacity the business was not using. The owner knew. Nobody had put a number on it.
What changed
Books rebuilt from source records, the leak cut, cash mapped forward, and the financing request rebuilt at the amount the business actually needed, then taken to more than one lender.
