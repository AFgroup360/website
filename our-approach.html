One idea, read from two sides of the table.

Better visibility and stronger financial control make a healthier business. That is worth the same to the owner and to whoever is funding them.

Jump to For business owners For capital providers Where the cash goes Get in touch

We are not there to police the business. We are there to make it legible.

The same monthly work produces both things. An owner who can see what is coming makes better calls. A lender looking at that business is reading numbers somebody stands behind.

Two perspectives

Same work. Different reason for wanting it.

For business owners

You get to decide with your eyes open.

You built the business. What you are missing is a current, forward view of what it is doing, and someone to talk it through with once a month.

  • Visibility. A number for next month, not just last month.
  • Cash flow control. The tight week seen while you can still act on it.
  • Better decisions. The hire, the equipment, the contract, priced properly.
  • Working capital discipline. Money freed from receivables and inventory.
  • Growth that the balance sheet can carry.
  • Financing readiness. Ready before you need to be.
For capital providers

You get numbers somebody stands behind.

You are backing a business you cannot see inside every day. We build the picture from source records and keep it current after the money goes out.

  • Confidence in the numbers. Rebuilt from bank statements, not summaries.
  • Downside visibility. What breaks first, and how early you would know.
  • Use of funds discipline. The money doing what the deal assumed.
  • Ongoing reporting on a set schedule.
  • Post funding monitoring for as long as the engagement runs.
  • Financial control around a business that is stronger for having it.
Where the cash goes

Most of it is just timing.

You pay the deposit in week one. The money shows up in week six. You are funding the stretch in between, and it widens as you grow.

What you notice first

The symptoms owners call us about.

  1. 01

    Cash is tight in a good month

    You paid before you got paid. The month was fine. The timing was not.

  2. 02

    The numbers arrive weeks late

    The books have not caught up to the revenue, so every decision is made on a guess.

  3. 03

    Nobody can say where the money went

    It is lumped into accounts nobody has broken out in years.

  4. 04

    A lender wants detail you do not have

    The books were built to file a tax return, not to support a credit decision.
