Better visibility makes a healthier business.

That is worth the same to the person running it and to the person funding it. The work underneath is largely the same work.

The idea

Visibility first. Control second. Decisions follow.

First

Visibility

Establish what is actually happening, from source records rather than from what was reported.

Then

Control

Put in the reporting, routines and controls that keep the picture current without anyone chasing it.

So that

Better decisions

Every call after that gets made against numbers that are real and current.

Two readings

Same work. Different reason for wanting it.

If you run the business

Control of the decision.

You know the business better than anyone. What is missing is a current view of what it is doing and someone to think it through with.

If you are funding one

Confidence in the position.

You are backing a business you cannot see inside every day. The same reporting gives you a reliable, decision useful view of it.

A better run business is the outcome either way. That is why we do not treat these as two different services.

What we look at

The seven things that tell you most.

Whatever the engagement, the diagnostic starts in the same places.

Cash

Not the balance today. What is committed, what is coming in, and where the position goes over the next weeks.

Working capital

The gap between paying suppliers and getting paid. Every new order is funded by the business before the customer funds it.

Reporting quality

Whether the numbers reconcile, arrive in time to be useful, and separate operating performance from everything else.

Debt capacity

What the business can service out of its own cash through a normal year, not a good one.

Financing readiness

Whether the position would stand up to someone outside the business reading it cold.

Use of funds

What the money is for, whether it is sized to the actual gap, and what it is expected to change.

Financial controls

Who approves what, how payments are run, and whether anything can move without somebody seeing it.

How a month runs

The same cycle, every month.

This is what keeps the numbers worth something on the day you need them.

  1. Through the month

    Monitor cash

    What cleared, what is about to, what is coming due.

  2. Month end

    Reconcile and close

    The books tied out and the month closed properly.

  3. Then

    Prepare reporting

    Performance against expectation, and where margin went.

  4. Together

    Review and decide

    The few things that actually need a decision.

  5. Before it restarts

    Update the forecast

    Rebuilt on what actually happened, not what was planned.

Then it begins again, with the forecast carrying forward what the month just taught it.

Why timing matters more than margin

Growth is funded before it is earned.

Win more work and you finance more of these gaps at the same time. The margin per job has not moved, but the cash required has.

It is the most common reason a profitable business ends up asking for money in a hurry, and it is visible months ahead if somebody is looking.
