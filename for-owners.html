What you actually get.

Every business is different. What it is worth is not.

Value one

Cash you can see coming.

The squeeze shows up weeks before it lands, not the morning it does. That is the difference between choosing what to do about it and absorbing it.

Value two

Money that stops leaking.

Spend that has never been questioned, found and sized. Most owners suspect it is there. Very few have a number for it, and you cannot cut what you cannot measure.

Value three

Capital on better terms.

Rate and structure follow the quality of the file. When the numbers hold up, financing becomes something you choose, at terms worth having, instead of something you scramble for.

Value four

Numbers you can run on.

Current, trusted, and in front of you every month. Not a year end document filed for the tax return, but a live picture you make decisions against.

Weekly

Monthly
Reconciled accounts, budget against actual, where cost and margin went.
As needed
Financing and sponsor packages, capital decisions, multi entity reporting.
