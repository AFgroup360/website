## Who We Serve

Intro: AmeriFinancial is built for owner-led businesses that have grown beyond
basic bookkeeping but don't yet need a full internal finance department.

**Typical client:** Established owner-led businesses that have outgrown
bookkeeping and need stronger cash visibility, management reporting, or

**Industries:**

- Inventory — Inventory-heavy businesses where margin discipline drives profit.
- Thin-margin operations where timing makes or breaks the month.
- Businesses Preparing for Lenders — Owners getting ready to face a lender or investor.

Caveat: The industries shown above are where we work most often — alongside
owner-led professional services, family businesses in transition and businesses
preparing for a bank facility, investor raise or sale. They are not the only
industries we serve.

### "Is AmeriFinancial the right fit?"

Weaker fit when:
- The business is pre-revenue or very early-stage. We work with operating businesses, not startups.
- The only need is bookkeeping or tax filing, where a bookkeeper or accountant is the right call, not us.
- The business is ready for a full internal finance team, which is a different stage.

Closer: A short conversation is the easiest way to find out. If we're not the
right partner, we'll tell you.

## FAQ — "Common Questions"

**How is AmeriFinancial different from a bookkeeper?**
A bookkeeper records what has already happened — essential work we don't
replace. AmeriFinancial works on what comes after the recording: forward cash
visibility, usable monthly reporting and financing readiness. Bookkeeping looks
back. AmeriFinancial looks forward.

**Do you replace my bookkeeper or accountant?**
No. Your bookkeeper keeps recording transactions and your accountant handles
year-end and tax. We sit between them.

**How does AmeriFinancial fit alongside an internal finance team?**
We work alongside any internal finance team, scoped to what the business
actually needs at each stage. Ongoing support is available where a business
needs continued financial control and reporting.

**Are you right for businesses preparing for a lender or investor?**
Yes, this is one of the clearest fits. We prepare the forecast, historical
financials, debt summary and supporting schedules the conversation requires. We
prepare the package — we don't broker or arrange financing.

**Who is the typical AmeriFinancial client?**
Typically, the business has outgrown basic bookkeeping but does not yet require
a full internal finance department.

**What does an engagement look like?**
A monthly rhythm of cash review, margin visibility and management reporting,
scaled to the size and stage of the business.

## For Lenders (new page, not from the previous site)

Everything above this line is recovered copy. This page is new, written from the
Farid/Adel working sessions. Flagged so it stays clear which copy is original to
the business and which was drafted here.

**Framing:** the lender initiates and is the reader. The borrower is the subject
of the analysis, not the audience.

**House style on this page:** no em-dashes. Flagged in review as reading as
machine-written. Commas, colons and full stops instead.

**H1:** Know what you are funding, and what happens to it after.

1. **What we check before you fund.** Source records (bank, credit card and
   operating line statements; accounting and bookkeeping data; filed returns and
   CRA position; balance sheet, income statement, debt stack and amounts
   outstanding), analysed bank statement led rather than bookkeeping led: 24
   months of trending cash flow, balance sheet depth and recoverability. Output:
   financeable or not, the amount that actually closes the gap, conditions to
   attach before funding, repayable structure and terms, a valuation view.
2. **What we watch every month after you fund.** Two halves. Keeping the
   position visible (cash forecast, obligations, ageing, deal conditions), and
   making the money work (working capital cycle, cash flow control, performance
   and valuation over the term).
3. **Most firms hand over a model and leave.** The continuity differentiator,
   with a stage table: before funding, at structuring, after funding.

Plus a section on the Financing Readiness Report, written as an umbrella
statement rather than a feature list: what was knowable at the time, put in
front of the lender and quantified.

### Confidentiality note

The source material (`AmeriFinancial_NPH_Advisory_Review.docx`) is marked
PRIVILEGED & CONFIDENTIAL, names the client and its owner, carries exact
figures, and states that the owner's represented revenue does not reconcile to
the filed statements. None of that is on the site, and none of it should be:
publishing it would breach the confidentiality undertaking in AmeriFinancial's
own privacy policy, and the revenue point is an allegation about an identifiable
third party.

What is published is the pattern only, with rounded and non-specific figures.

To run it as a named case study, get the client's written sign-off first. With
that in hand the real name, sector and numbers can be swapped in, and it becomes
a considerably stronger page.
