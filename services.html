Our services

Financial control built around the needs of the business.

We build a finance process around what actually drives your business. Four areas, and how deep we go into each one depends on you.

One

Cash flow control

Forward visibility into cash, obligations and liquidity. Where the money is going, when payments land, and what is coming due before it comes due.

Two

Management reporting

A monthly close you can rely on, with performance against what you expected, statements that reconcile, and a review call to go through it.

Three

Receivables, payables and working capital

Who owes you, who you owe, what is sitting in inventory, and how the timing between them is affecting cash.

Four

Financing readiness

We review the position behind the request, surface what would slow a lender down, and organise the analysis for management. We prepare the package. We don't broker or arrange the financing.

The ongoing work

It is a monthly job, not a project.

A one-off report goes stale. Most of what we do is the standing rhythm underneath it, so the numbers are current on the day you need them.

  1. Through the month

    We watch the bank as it moves. What cleared, what is about to, and what is coming due before it comes due. If something is going to be tight, you hear it from us with weeks to work with, not on the day.

  2. At close

    The books get reconciled and the month gets closed properly. Performance against what you expected, where the margin went, and what moved on the balance sheet.

  3. The review call

    We sit down and go through it with you. Not a pack in your inbox. The three or four things that actually need a decision, and what we would do about them.

  4. Then it starts again

    The forecast gets rebuilt on what actually happened, receivables and payables get worked, and the file stays current. So when a lender, a buyer or an opportunity turns up, nothing has to be reconstructed.

The deliverable

The Financing Readiness Report.

One document that says what the business is actually worth backing, and what it would take.

Most owners have never seen their own business written up the way a lender or a buyer sees it. That is the gap this closes. We rebuild the position from the raw records, put it in one document, and hand it to you before anybody else asks for it.

You do not need to be borrowing for it to be worth having. It tells you where the cash actually goes, what the balance sheet really looks like, and which problems are costing you money right now. Then when the day comes that you are raising, refinancing or selling, the work is already done and it is the same number either way.

Request a sample report →

What is inside it.

  • A verdict, on the first page Financeable, not financeable, or financeable on conditions. Stated plainly, with the conditions listed, so nobody has to read thirty pages to find the answer.
  • Two years of cash flow, month by month Operating cash separated from owner and related-party movement, so you can see what the business actually produces and what was carrying it.
  • The obligations, all of them Every loan, lease, tax arrear and supplier balance, with amounts, terms and where each one sits in the queue.
  • What the money would be used for Sized against the gap we measured, not against what someone hoped to ask for, and set out so a credit team can follow it.
  • What the business is worth, and what changes it Valuation logic and a pro forma showing the position after the conditions are met. This is the part that matters in a sale.

Every figure is traced back to a bank statement, and every open question is put to management and answered in writing before the report goes out. What cannot be tied out is disclosed rather than smoothed over.
