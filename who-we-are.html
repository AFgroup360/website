What we have done

Three engagements, told plainly.

  1. 01

    A systems supplier preparing for private financing

    We rebuilt 24 months of bank activity and tied 92 percent of it back to source statements. Every one of 186 open questions was answered by management in writing before the report went out, and the verdict sat on page one.

    The private lender who read that review then engaged us on its own investor materials.

  2. 02

    A food producer under lender pressure

    We held the restructuring seat and ran the finance side day to day. Cash came under control, the lenders knew where things stood, and monthly reporting started landing on time.

    A family business with no finance function

    We built the finance department from nothing, then realigned supplier terms so money going out matched money coming in. The interest bill came down.

Common questions

The three we get asked most.

How are you different from a bookkeeper?

A bookkeeper records what already happened, and we do not replace that. We work on what comes after. Where cash is heading, a report you can act on, and being ready when you need to borrow.

Do you replace my accountant?

No. Your accountant handles year end and tax. We sit between them and the decisions you make during the year.

What does a month look like?

We watch the bank through the month, close the books properly, then go through the numbers with you on a call. Smaller businesses get a lighter version of the same rhythm.
