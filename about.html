About

Why AmeriFinancial exists.

AmeriFinancial was created to give business owners the financial leadership, insight and guidance they need to make better decisions and grow at a pace the business can carry.

We work alongside owners and their leadership teams to improve profitability, strengthen cash flow, sharpen financial visibility, and build confidence in the decisions that shape the future of the business.

Every owner should be able to answer three things on any given day. Where the cash is, what it is doing, and what happens next. Our job is to make sure they can.

Founder

Farid Ameri

Farid has spent his career on both sides of the financing table. He has run finance from inside businesses under real pressure, built finance departments where none existed, and worked on the credit side of a major bank deciding which files got funded and which did not.

That work has spanned manufacturing, food production, distribution and services, in Canada and abroad. The job underneath it never changes. Get the cash under control, get the numbers straight, then make the decision. It is why an owner gets a straight answer here, and why a capital provider gets a file that holds up.
