Who we serve

Two sides of the same table.

An owner deciding whether the business can carry the money. A lender or investor deciding whether to put it in. It is the same question, asked from opposite chairs, and the same set of numbers answers both.

Why both

The same work, read from either chair.

Owners and capital providers are asking one question in two forms. Can this business carry the money, and will it still be able to a year from now.

We build the answer the same way for both: from bank statements and source records rather than from what has been reported, refreshed month after month instead of once at the start. The owner uses it to decide what to take on. The capital provider uses it to decide what to put in, and to see what happens next.
