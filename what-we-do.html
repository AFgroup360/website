For capital providers

Before the money goes in, and after.

The same financial control work, applied first to understanding the business and then to keeping it running well.

Before funding

Understand the business.

A Financing Readiness Review, built from the company's own source records rather than from what has been reported.

  • The real financial position
  • Financial risks surfaced early
  • Financeability and affordability
  • Debt and obligations, with terms and priority
  • What the financing is actually for
  • Conditions that may need addressing first

After funding

Keep it visible and controlled.

Much of what we do for owners, because the objective is still that the underlying business does well.

  • Recurring management reporting
  • Cash flow monitoring
  • Working capital oversight
  • Financial controls maintained
  • Ongoing financial visibility
  • Updates on how the business is performing

This is not oversight of the borrower. A business with better financial control is a stronger business, which is what both sides wanted from the start.

The deliverable

The Financing Readiness Review.

One document stating where the business stands and what would have to be true for capital to go in. It reaches a verdict rather than leaving the reader to form one.

What is inside it.

The period reviewed is set by the business and what the financing is for, not by a fixed template.

01

The verdict

Financeable, financeable on conditions, or not financeable in the current position. Stated on the first page.

02

Historical cash position and operating cash flow

What the business actually generated over the period reviewed, with owner and related party movement separated out.

03

Working capital position

Receivables, payables, inventory and the gap the business funds between paying out and getting paid.

04

Debt and obligations

Loans, leases, arrears and supplier balances, with amounts, terms, and where each one sits in the queue.

05

What the financing is for

The use of funds, sized against the gap we measured rather than the number that was hoped for.

06

Affordability

What the business can service out of its own cash through a normal year, not a good one.

07

Conditions

What a lender would reasonably attach, set out before they attach it, so it can be dealt with first.

08

What is confirmed, and what is still open

Figures tied back to source documents with the method stated. Anything that could not be tied out is disclosed, not smoothed over.
