The work that sits between bookkeeping and the decision.

Four areas. How deep we go in each one depends on what the business actually needs.

Jump to The four areas The review How a month runs Get in touch
  1. 01

    Cash flow control

    We watch the bank as it moves and keep a forward view of what is coming due. If a month is going to be tight you hear it from us with weeks to work with, not on the Friday payroll does not clear.

    Most owners have a number for last month. Very few have one for next month.

  2. 02

    Management reporting

    The books get reconciled and the month gets closed properly. You get performance against what you expected, where the margin went, and what moved on the balance sheet.

    Then we sit down and go through it. Three or four things that need a decision, and what we would do about them.

  3. 03

    Receivables, payables and working capital

    Who owes you, who you owe, and what is tied up in inventory. We work the timing between them, because that gap is where growth quietly eats the cash a good month produced.

  4. 04

    Financing readiness

    We review the position behind the request and surface what would slow a lender down, before anyone else finds it. We prepare the package. We do not broker or arrange the financing.

A report that is three months old is history, not control.

This is a standing monthly job. That is what makes the numbers worth anything on the day you need them.

The deliverable

The Financing Readiness Review.

One document that says whether the business is worth backing, and what it would take.

Download the sample review

What is inside it.

  • Verdict on page oneFinanceable, not financeable, or financeable on conditions, with the conditions listed.
  • Two years of cashMonth by month, with operating cash separated from what was carrying it.
  • Every dollar you oweLoans, leases, tax arrears and supplier balances, with terms and priority.
  • What the money is forSized against the gap we measured, not the number someone hoped to ask for.
  • Conditions a lender would attachWhat has to be true before the money goes out.

Every figure traces back to a bank statement. What cannot be tied out is disclosed rather than smoothed over.
