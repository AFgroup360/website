For lenders

The financial picture behind the borrower — before you fund, and every month after.

AmeriFinancial works with owner-led businesses on cash-flow visibility, management reporting and financial control. When those businesses approach a lender, we prepare the analysis behind the request — and keep it current once the facility is in place.

01

What we check before they fund.

Before a request reaches you, we work through the position behind it and surface what would come up in credit review anyway.

  • A forecast built from actual cash timing, not a growth assumption
  • The debt and obligation schedule, including what sits off the balance sheet
  • Working capital: receivables ageing, payables pressure, inventory tied up
  • The quality of the records the numbers are drawn from
  • Customer and supplier concentration
  • Where the covenant headroom actually is

Issues that would surface in credit review are identified before the file reaches you, not after.

02

What we watch every month after they fund.

The risk does not end at close. Monthly finance control keeps the position visible for the life of the facility.

  • A rolling 13-week cash forecast, refreshed on a fixed cycle
  • Covenant position tracked against the agreement, not estimated at quarter-end
  • Receivables and payables ageing, month over month
  • Actual performance against the forecast that supported the request
  • Obligations coming due, before they come due
  • Reporting in a format built for review, not reconstruction

Deterioration shows up in the reporting early, while there are still options.

03

Why the borrower's own bookkeeper cannot do this.

This is not a criticism of bookkeepers. The work is essential and we don't replace it. It is a different job.

Different direction

A bookkeeper records what has already happened. Lender monitoring is a forward view — what cash is available next quarter, and whether the covenant holds.

Different scope

Bookkeeping is scoped and priced to keep the records accurate. It is not scoped to build forecasts, track covenants, or prepare analysis in the form a credit file requires.

Different output

Accurate records are an input to this work, not a substitute for it. The books being current does not mean the position behind them has been examined.

Bookkeeping looks back. This work looks forward.

04 · Independence

We work for neither side of the table.

The analysis does not change depending on who is reading it. AmeriFinancial does not broker or arrange financing, and takes no position in the outcome of a lending decision.
